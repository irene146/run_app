Marathon Coach

London 2027 · adaptive plan built around 3 core runs, Tuesday swim, Wednesday climbing, and your treadmill-only ship block.
Training principle
Three core runs are the default. Optional runs are only added when recovery is good. During the ship block (28 Jan–28 Mar), swimming and climbing disappear and the plan switches to treadmill-only training.
Your data
Logs are stored only in this browser on this device. Export a backup occasionally, especially before clearing Safari data or changing phones.
Pace is guidance, not a test. For easy and long runs, conversational effort overrides the displayed pace. If pain changes your gait, worsens as you run, or persists, stop and seek appropriate clinical advice rather than using the app to push through it.
